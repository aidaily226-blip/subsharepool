## What Is Steam Families?

Steam Families is Steam's official game sharing system. It was updated in March 2024, combining the older Steam Family Sharing and Steam Family View features into one unified system.

Here is what it allows you to do in simple words:

- Share your entire Steam game library with up to **5 other people**
- Each person plays on their own Steam account — separate saves, achievements, and progress
- Up to **6 members total** in one Steam Family (including yourself)
- Members can play your games even when you are online — as long as you are not playing the same game at the same time
- Completely free — no extra cost to set up

Steam Families allows users to create a Steam family of up to six members to share games and implement content controls. Anyone in the Steam family can play any shared game in the library, as long as another member is not playing it already.

This is 100% legal and officially supported by Valve. It is not a loophole or a workaround. Steam built this feature specifically so that families and trusted friends can share games without buying multiple copies.

## Step-by-Step — How to Set Up Steam Families

Here is exactly how to set it up. This works on Windows, Mac, and Linux.

### Step 1 — Enable Steam Guard on Your Account

Steam Guard is a security feature that protects your account. You must have it enabled before you can use Steam Families.

- Open the Steam app on your computer
- Click **Steam** in the top left corner
- Click **Settings**
- Click **Account**
- Click **Manage Steam Guard Account Security**
- Make sure Steam Guard is turned ON

If it is already on, skip to Step 2.

### Step 2 — Open Family Settings

- In Steam, click **Steam** then **Settings**
- Click the **Family** tab on the left side
- You will see the Steam Families section

### Step 3 — Create Your Steam Family

- Click **Create a Steam Family**
- Give your family a name — it can be anything you like
- Click **Create**

You are now the adult manager of the family group.

### Step 4 — Invite Members

- Click **Invite a Member**
- Search for the Steam username or enter the email address of the person you want to invite
- Choose whether they are an **Adult** or a **Child** in the family
- Click **Send Invite**

The other person will receive an invitation on their Steam account. Once they accept, they immediately get access to your shared library.

You can add up to 5 other people — 6 total including yourself.

### Step 5 — Start Playing

Once a member accepts your invitation, they can:

- Open their own Steam account
- Go to their Library
- See all the games from your shared library available to download and play
- Download any game and play it on their own account
- Save their own progress completely separately from yours

That is it. The whole setup takes about 5 minutes.

## Important Rules and Limits

Steam Families is generous but it does have some rules you need to know before you start.

**Two people CAN play at the same time — different games**
This is a newer update to Steam Families. Two family members can be playing at the same time as long as they are playing different games. If two people want to play the same game at the same moment, one of them needs their own purchased copy.

**Cannot share DLC separately**
If you own a game but not its DLC, your family members can play the base game but not the DLC content.

**Some games cannot be shared**
Games that require a separate third-party subscription or online service may have restrictions. Games that require EA Play, Ubisoft Connect subscriptions, or are free-to-play cannot be shared via Steam Families.

**Region restrictions may apply**
Some games have regional licensing restrictions. A game available in your country may not be playable by a family member in a different country due to publisher agreements.

**Each person keeps their own saves and achievements**
Your progress is completely separate from everyone else in the family. Nobody can overwrite your game saves or take your achievements.

**You can only be in one Steam Family at a time**
Each Steam account can only belong to one Steam Family. You cannot split your library across multiple groups.

## Common Questions About Steam Families

**Can I share games with someone in another country?**
Yes in most cases. Steam Families works across different countries. However some games have regional restrictions set by their publishers that may limit playability in certain regions.

**Can someone in my family buy games that I can access too?**
Yes — this is one of the best features. Every member of the Steam Family shares their library with everyone else. So if 5 people each buy 10 different games, all 5 people potentially have access to 50 games total.

**What happens if I remove someone from my Steam Family?**
They immediately lose access to your shared library. They keep their own purchased games and their own saves from games they played.

**Is Steam Families free to use?**
Yes — completely free. You just need a Steam account and games in your library. No subscription required.

**Can family members play offline?**
Yes. Once a game is downloaded, family members can play it in offline mode without needing an internet connection.

**How many people can be in one Steam Family?**
Up to 6 people total — including yourself. You can invite up to 5 other people.
